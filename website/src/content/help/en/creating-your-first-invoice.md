## Draft the invoice

1. Go to **Invoices** → **New invoice**.
2. Pick the customer (add them first under **Contacts** if they're new).
3. Add line items with descriptions and amounts in MWK.
4. Choose a due date. Ledgr marks invoices overdue automatically.

## Brand it

Your business name, logo, address and TPIN appear on the invoice automatically
from your business profile. Set your brand colour in Settings → Business
Profile — it themes the whole document.

## Send it

Send the invoice by email straight from Ledgr (delivered via SendGrid), or
download the PDF and share it any way you like.

## Track it

Every invoice has an open-tracking pixel: you'll know when the customer has
seen it, and payment status updates automatically when it's paid.
