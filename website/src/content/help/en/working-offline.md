## Install Ledgr as an app

- **Android (Chrome):** open Ledgr, tap the ⋮ menu → **Install app**.
- **iPhone (Safari):** open Ledgr, tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**.

Installed, Ledgr behaves like a native app and loads instantly.

## When you lose connection

Keep working normally. Income, expenses, invoices and journal entries are
saved to an offline queue on your device. The queue shows a pending count.

## When you reconnect

Ledgr syncs automatically — you can watch the queue drain. Conflicts are
resolved deterministically, so both your device and the server end up with the
same books.

## Tips

- Check the sync indicator before switching devices.
- The dashboard works from cached data offline — reports may be limited to
  what's cached.
- Low bandwidth? Ledgr only transfers what changed, so it uses very little data.
