## Create your account

Sign up with your email — no card required. Confirm your email address and
sign in.

## Set up your business profile

1. Choose **Create business**.
2. Enter your business name, trading name (if any), and details like TPIN and
   address. These appear on your invoices.
3. Pick a brand color — it themes the whole app.

## Your chart of accounts

Ledgr seeds a standard chart of accounts for Malawian businesses. You can
customise it any time under **Accounts**.

## Record your first transaction

From the dashboard, choose **Record income** or **Record expense**. Enter the
amount in MWK, pick an account, and save. That's it — your first entry is
journalised automatically.

## Next steps

- Invite your bookkeeper or accountant (Settings → Team)
- Create your first invoice (Invoices → New)
- Install Ledgr as an app so it works offline
