## Add your employees

Go to **Payroll** → **Employees** → **Add employee**. Enter their name,
monthly gross pay and any allowances or deductions.

## Run a pay run

1. Choose the period (e.g. July 2026).
2. Ledgr computes each employee's PAYE from the current bands, plus pension
   contributions and any withholding tax.
3. Review the summary — gross, deductions, net pay — then approve.

## Payslips

Each employee gets a payslip you can print or email. Payslips are stored with
the pay run, so you always have the history when MRA or a bank asks.

## Filing

The monthly payroll report gives you the totals you need for PAYE remittance
and pension fund submissions. Keep it with your records for the year.
