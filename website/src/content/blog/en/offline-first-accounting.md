When the lights go out, your business doesn't stop — your records shouldn't
either.

## The problem with cloud-only software

Most accounting tools assume a stable connection. In Malawi, that assumption
fails daily: load-shedding, patchy 3G, expensive data. The result? Business
owners either queue their bookkeeping for later (and forget) or keep a second
set of records that never matches.

## Offline-first, not offline-only

An offline-first app downloads what you need, records your transactions on
your device, and syncs quietly when a connection returns. You never lose a
sale because the network was down.

## What to look for

- **Installable** — works from your home screen like a native app
- **Queued writes** — transactions saved locally, synced later
- **Low data usage** — syncs only what changed
