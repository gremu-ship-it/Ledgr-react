If you employ staff in Malawi, you are probably a PAYE collector whether you
realise it or not. Here is what you need to know — in plain language.

## What is PAYE?

PAYE (Pay As You Earn) is the income tax deducted from employees' salaries
before they receive them. As an employer you withhold it and remit it to the
Malawi Revenue Authority each month.

## How the bands work

Malawi uses a progressive PAYE structure: the first portion of monthly pay is
tax-free, and higher portions are taxed at increasing rates. The exact bands
change with the national budget, so always check the current MRA schedule.

## Your obligations as an employer

1. **Register** with MRA as an employer.
2. **Deduct PAYE** from each employee each month, per the bands.
3. **Remit** the withheld tax to MRA by the deadline.
4. **Keep records** — payslips, payroll summaries and proof of remittance.
