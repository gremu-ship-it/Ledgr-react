Filing season is stressful when your books aren't ready. This checklist covers
the basics every Malawian small business should have in place before filing.

## 1. Your records are complete

Every income and expense for the period is recorded — bank transfers, cash,
mobile money. If you use Ledgr, your journal balances are integrity-checked
automatically.

## 2. VAT is calculated correctly

Output VAT on sales, input VAT on purchases. Ledgr computes VAT on each
transaction so your return ties back to your ledger line by line.

## 3. Payroll deductions reconcile

PAYE, pension and any withholding tax deducted from employees must match what
you remitted. Run a payroll report for the period and check totals before
filing.

## 4. You have the reports at hand

Export your profit & loss and balance sheet — you will often need them to
answer queries. Ledgr reports are exportable in one click.

## Why this matters

Businesses that file late or with errors face penalties and audits. Keeping
books throughout the year — instead of reconstructing them in a panic — is the
single biggest thing you can do to make filing easy.

*This is general guidance, not tax advice. Confirm requirements with MRA or
your accountant.*
